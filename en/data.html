Kazakhstan key business data

Get a few key numbers straight before you commit. Below we organise the figures into market, tax, AIFC and logistics, with the source basis noted for each — always verify against the latest official publications.

In one sentence

Kazakhstan is the gateway to Central Asia for Chinese companies: corporate income tax is generally 20%, VAT is 12%, the AIFC offers English common law and tax incentives, and the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) covers a market of about 183 million people.
