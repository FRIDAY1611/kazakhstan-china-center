Entering Kazakhstan — the questions, answered

The 25 questions most clients ask before booking: market judgement, delegation arrangements, registration structures, visas and people, legal and tax, logistics, and confidentiality. Answers are kept plain, including what we cannot do. Anything not covered here — just book a consultation.

Common questions about entering Kazakhstan — market, delegations, registration, visas, tax, logistics and confidentiality — answered in one place; for a route tailored to your case, book a consultation.

All questions

25 answers, grouped by topic

Is now a good time for Chinese companies to enter the Kazakhstan market?
It depends on your industry and product. Energy, mining, agriculture, auto parts, agricultural machinery, building materials, consumer electronics and cross-border logistics are closely linked to the Chinese supply chain, while localisation requirements (licences, certification, employment, tax) are tightening. We recommend an eligibility assessment before committing; we do not promise guaranteed success, only clear constraints and a clear path.
I know nothing about Kazakhstan. What should the first step be?
Start with an eligibility assessment: whether your product needs a licence or certification, who your target customers are, which city to enter from, and whether you have a local partner. It usually takes 1–2 weeks to reach a conclusion before registration and investment.
Almaty or Astana first?
Almaty is the commercial and consumer centre for trade, retail, logistics and private business; Astana is the political and administrative centre, better for ministries, investment agencies and large state-owned projects. Most companies first see the market in Almaty, then arrange government meetings in Astana.
Which industries are easier to enter?
There is no guaranteed sector. Relatively easier starts are industries strongly linked to the Chinese supply chain with clear local demand: auto parts and agricultural machinery, building materials, consumer electronics and home goods, food-processing equipment, and cross-border logistics. Heavy-asset or heavily regulated sectors need longer licence cycles and heavier local compliance.
What is the difference between a representative office, a branch and an independent company?
A representative office can only conduct liaison and market research; a branch depends on its overseas parent; an independent legal entity (local LLP/ТОО or an AIFC entity) can sign, invoice, employ and receive payments locally. If you plan to actually operate, tender or sell locally, you normally need an independent legal entity.
How many days is a delegation, and how is it arranged?
A standard delegation is 4–5 days, based in Almaty with 1–2 additional days in Astana as needed. It typically includes a policy and market briefing, institutional visits, B2B matchmaking, and visits to industrial parks and representative companies. Needs profiling and bookings are completed 2–3 weeks before departure.
What level of people can a delegation meet?
It depends on the industry, agenda and lead time. Common meetings are department or bureau-level officials, heads of investment promotion bodies, and executives of business associations and local companies. Ministerial meetings need longer lead time and a concrete cooperation agenda; we state feasibility honestly without over-promising.
How many people make a group? Can it be customised?
Usually 8–15 people, with around 10 giving the best one-on-one matchmaking experience. Fewer than 8 or special needs (single-industry deep dives, small executive groups) can be customised, though the per-person cost rises.
What does the delegation fee include and exclude?
We provide an itemised quotation before signing. It usually includes itinerary and booking, local transport, interpreting and accompaniment, meeting organisation and materials; international flights, visa fees, accommodation and meals are typically settled at cost or by the client, per the quotation.
Do I have to be in Kazakhstan in person to register a company?
Most registration formalities can be handled by an authorised representative, but bank account opening, some licences and interviews may require the legal representative to be present. It depends on company type, bank and scope; we flag the must-attend steps during the structuring assessment.
AIFC structure or local company — how to choose?
Look at your client base, capital route and governance needs. For local government, state-owned or local customers, a local LLP (ТОО) is smoother; as a holding or investment platform needing English-language governance and international arbitration, AIFC fits better. Choosing wrong means annual compliance cost, so assess first.
How fast can I get a business licence?
Registering the entity itself usually takes 2–6 weeks, but having a licence is not the same as being able to operate — the bank account, tax registration and sector licences are often the critical path. We schedule against "able to actually operate", not simply completing registration.
Why is bank account opening difficult? What should I prepare?
Banks must complete anti-money-laundering and sanctions compliance due diligence on your business model, source of funds, beneficial owner and expected transactions. You usually need business evidence, corporate and beneficial-owner documents, source-of-funds statements, and expected volumes and counterparties. Pre-communicate with the target bank before registering.
Is there a registered capital requirement?
Requirements differ by entity type and industry. Local LLPs have a general minimum registered capital, with additional thresholds in some licensed sectors; AIFC entities follow their own rules. The exact amount and payment schedule are confirmed during the structuring assessment under current regulations.
After registering, can bookkeeping and tax filing be done remotely?
Day-to-day bookkeeping and filings can be done remotely, but they must be executed by local accountants under Kazakh accounting standards and the tax calendar. Through our partner accounting firms we provide bookkeeping, filing and annual compliance with Chinese-speaking coordination.
How do business visa, work permit and residence connect?
The typical path is: enter on a business visa for inspection and preparation, apply for a work permit after the company is set up, then obtain temporary residence on the basis of the work permit. A business visa cannot replace a work permit; long-term postings must follow the work-permit and residence procedures.
Are there limits on foreign employees?
Yes. Kazakhstan sets local-staff ratio and quota requirements for some industries and entity types, which must be met when applying for work permits. Specific ratios vary by industry, region and regulation, and are applied under the rules in force at the time of application.
Can family members come along?
After the company is set up and the work permit and residence are obtained, spouses and minor children can usually apply for accompanying residence, with materials and timing depending on the individual case. We provide the checklist when processing.
How do you charge for your services?
Each project is assessed individually; we provide a written quotation and scope of work with clearly defined deliverables before signing. Government fees and third-party costs are listed separately, and we do not charge vague fees based on success.
What is your relationship with local law and accounting firms?
We are the project coordinator and Chinese-speaking interface; professional legal, audit and tax opinions are issued by partner firms with local qualifications. The partner list and their credentials are available on request.
What are the most common tax pitfalls?
Treating incentives as exemptions; ignoring VAT registration and invoicing; lacking transfer-pricing documentation for cross-border related-party transactions; and not filing employment and social contributions under local rules. Early non-compliance tends to surface during annual review or audit.
Rail or cross-border trucking?
Bulk, time-insensitive cargo is more economical by rail; inland, fragmented or time-sensitive cargo is more flexible by truck. Rail-plus-truck intermodal via ports like Khorgos balances cost and lead time, assessed by cargo type, season and port congestion.
How long does EAC certification take?
It depends on the product category and certification form, from weeks to months, with some products needing sample testing and factory audit. Schedule certification into your go-to-market timeline rather than waiting until cargo reaches the border.
Can I use just one service?
Yes. The six modules can be started individually or run together. Most clients start with an eligibility assessment or a business delegation, then add registration, visas and logistics as needed.
How is my commercial information kept confidential?
A non-disclosure agreement can be signed before we start; project materials circulate only within the project team, and we obtain your consent before disclosing to partner institutions. See the Privacy Policy (Chinese) for how website form data is handled.
