Six service modules covering the full market-entry cycle

We are not an information broker. Every module is delivered by a team based in Kazakhstan, connecting you with people who can actually make decisions. Below we spell out what each module delivers, how it runs and roughly how long it takes — so you know what you get before you book.

Core Services

From the first market visit to the first contract

Most companies enter Kazakhstan in this order: first assess whether it is feasible, then decide how to set up, then solve the people and cargo questions. The six modules can be started individually or run together as one project.

01 · Government Relations

Government Relations & Investment Advisory

Policy briefingEligibilityInstitutional access

For companies preparing to enter the Kazakh market but not yet decided on a city or investment structure. We translate policy language into executable steps: what access restrictions apply to your industry, which region fits best, which institutions you can meet, and what materials to prepare next.

Deliverables

  • Industry access and policy briefing
  • City / industrial park comparison
  • Project roadmap and document checklist
  • Target institutions and meeting arrangements

Typical timeline

  • Initial assessment: 1–2 weeks
  • In-depth advisory + institutional meetings: 2–4 weeks
  • Subject to the level of engagement and approval pace
02 · Business Delegations

Business Delegations & Matchmaking

Custom itinerariesInstitutional visitsB2B matchmaking

Not a sightseeing tour. We profile your needs before the trip, arrange one-on-one meetings and institutional visits during it, and deliver an actionable project list afterwards — the real work begins when the delegation ends.

Deliverables

  • Custom itinerary and visit schedule
  • Government / business meeting bookings and interpreting
  • B2B matchmaking meetings
  • Post-trip follow-up pipeline

Typical timeline

  • Preparation: 2–3 weeks
  • Delegation: 3–5 days (Almaty / Astana)
  • Follow-up: ongoing
03 · Company Formation

Company Formation & Bank Accounts

AIFC structureLocal companyBank account

Choose the structure first, then register. The AIFC structure and the local limited liability company (ТОО) differ notably in applicable law, language, tax and account opening; choosing wrong creates long-term compliance cost. We assess first, then implement.

Deliverables

  • Structure assessment and registration path
  • Name check, charter and registration documents
  • Tax registration, registered address, corporate secretary
  • Bank account documentation and accompaniment

Typical timeline

  • Registration: about 2–6 weeks (depends on type and documents)
  • Bank account: about 1–4 weeks (depends on KYC)
  • Subject to approvals and the bank's actual pace
04 · Visas & Permits

Visas & Work Permits

Business visasWork permitsResidence permits

If people cannot get in, the project does not move. Business invitations, work permits and temporary residence involve long chains and detailed document requirements. We list documents per person and per case, and track every milestone so paperwork never costs you a negotiation or a production start.

Deliverables

  • Visa category assessment and document checklist
  • Business invitation processing
  • Work permit and residence permit follow-through
  • Expiry reminders and renewal support

Typical timeline

  • Business visa: about 1–3 weeks
  • Work permit: about 3–8 weeks
  • Subject to migration and labour authority approvals
05 · Legal & Tax

Legal & Tax

Compliance reviewContract reviewTax planning

Executed by local law and accounting firms, coordinated and managed by us in Chinese. The point is not calling a lawyer after trouble starts, but closing risks before signing, hiring, paying and filing tax returns.

Deliverables

  • Compliance review and remediation advice
  • Chinese–Russian bilingual contract review and revision
  • Tax filing and planning support
  • Employment and dispute resolution support

Typical timeline

  • Contract review: 3–7 business days
  • Compliance review: 2–4 weeks
  • Assessed per matter complexity
06 · Logistics & Customs

Logistics & Customs

RailCross-border truckingEAC certification

Cargo reaching the border is not cargo reaching your customer. We compare the time and cost of rail versus cross-border trucking, handle customs clearance and certification, and report milestones in transit — turning "when can you deliver?" into a question you can answer.

Deliverables

  • Transport options and quotation comparison
  • Customs declaration documents and execution
  • EAC certification coordination
  • In-transit tracking and exception handling

Typical timeline

  • Options and quotation: 3–7 business days
  • Transit time: assessed per route and mode
  • Certification: assessed per product category

FAQ

Questions you may have before booking

I know nothing about Kazakhstan. What should the first step be?
Start with an eligibility assessment rather than rushing to register a company: whether your product needs a licence locally, who your target customers are, and which city is the best entry point. This usually takes 1–2 weeks and determines everything that follows. You can book a requirements interview directly.
Do I have to be in Kazakhstan in person to register a company?
Most registration formalities can be handled by an authorised representative, but bank account opening, some licences and in-person interviews may require the legal representative to be present. It depends on the company type, bank and scope of business; we flag the must-attend steps during the structuring assessment.
How fast can I obtain a business licence?
Registering the entity usually takes 2–6 weeks, but having a licence is not the same as being able to operate — the bank account, tax registration and sector licences are often the critical path. We schedule against "able to actually operate", not simply completing registration.
How do you charge for your services?
Each project is assessed individually. We provide a written quotation and scope of work, with deliverables clearly defined, before signing. We do not charge vague success fees; government fees and third-party costs are listed separately.
What is your relationship with local law and accounting firms?
We are the project coordinator and Chinese-speaking interface. Professional legal, audit and tax opinions are issued by partner firms with local qualifications. The list of partner firms and credentials are available on request.
How is my commercial information kept confidential?
A non-disclosure agreement can be signed before we start; project materials circulate only within the project team, and we obtain your consent before disclosing anything to partner institutions. See the Privacy Policy (Chinese) for how website form data is handled.
